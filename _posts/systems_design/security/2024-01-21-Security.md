A sequential learning path for system design security, from first principles to
enterprise-grade advanced topics. Articles are grouped into phases — each phase
builds on the previous one.

## Reading tips

- Phases 1–4 are prerequisites for everything after them — don't skip ahead.
- Phase 5 (OAuth/SSO) is the most interview-heavy section; read it with the
  JWT article fresh in mind.
- Phases 6–9 can be read in any order once 1–5 are done.
